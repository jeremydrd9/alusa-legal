What Alusa is

Alusa is a calorie tracking app with an AI body analyzer. You photograph a meal and receive an estimate of its calories and macronutrients. You photograph yourself and receive an estimate of your physique development over time.

Alusa is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Nothing in the app is a diagnosis, a treatment, or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Do not use Alusa to make medical decisions. If you have or suspect an eating disorder, a metabolic condition, or any other health concern, speak to a doctor. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or under medical supervision, consult your doctor before changing what you eat.

Estimates are estimates

Calorie and macronutrient figures produced from a photograph are approximations. They depend on what is visible, on portion size, and on ingredients the camera cannot see. Treat them as a useful signal over time, not as exact measurements.

Body scan scores describe training development only. They are not a health assessment, not a fitness-for-activity assessment, and not a judgement of appearance. They are estimated from photographs and carry real uncertainty. The trend across several scans is more meaningful than any single number.
